# B02 · 8컷 시트 1장 + 16컷 시트 2장 (연필 콘티) — sheet_8, sheet_16_1, sheet_16_2

**첨부:** 첨부 없음. 16컷 시트 2는 같은 대화에서 시트 1 결과를 첨부

**Genspark에 먼저 붙여 넣기 ↓**
```
콘티 북 페이지에 넣을 연필 콘티 시트를 한 장씩 차례로 만들어 줘.
- 비율 16:9, 가로 1920px 이상
- 흑백 연필 스케치, 인물은 동그라미 머리와 십자선의 단순한 마네킹이지만 실루엣 규칙으로 누구인지 알아볼 수 있게
- 등장인물은 모두 성인. 잠옷은 단정한 긴소매
- 그림 안에 글자·숫자·컷 번호 금지(빈 틀과 빈 줄은 허용)
- 둘째 장부터는 첨부한 앞 시트와 같은 그림체·선 굵기·실루엣으로
```

```

The figures are adult proportions (about 7.5 heads tall).
Eight panels in two rows of four, read left to right, top to bottom. Story: a lab assistant's day from waking up at home to finishing a storyboard meeting at the lab.
Panel 1 (cut 1): wide shot: a morning bedroom, Haru waking up and stretching.
Panel 2 (cut 2): full body: Haru in her lab coat stepping out of the front door toward the right.
Panel 3 (cut 3): chest-up: Haru on a bus by the window, looking up to the upper right.
Panel 4 (cut 4): low angle full body: Haru arriving at the research institute gate.
Panel 5 (cut 5): waist-up: the Professor, Sia and Moa greeting Haru in the lobby.
Panel 6 (cut 6): wide shot: everyone gathered around Haru in the lab.
Panel 7 (cut 7): waist-up: the Professor at a whiteboard, the team around the table.
Panel 8 (cut 8): close-up: Haru looking up to the upper right with determination.
Keep every character recognizable by silhouette even in rough sketch form: Haru = slim adult woman with two round hair buns on top of her head and a long open lab coat; the Professor = taller adult man with spiky hair and rectangular glasses; Sia = adult woman with long straight hair, no accessories; Moa = adult woman with wavy hair and a headband with a small ribbon on her LEFT side; AbaBot = boxy monitor-head robot on tank treads with a short antenna; the Fruit Fly = tiny insect with round eyes, a helmet and wings; the judges = three small round figures (one with a side bow, one with a beret, one with a cat-ear hood).
```
